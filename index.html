File 01 // The Ravencraft Family

It started with Barry, in a house that should have stayed empty.

In the winter of 1971, seven-year-old Barry Ravencraft shared a farmhouse hallway with something the family called the cold spot — a patch of floor that stayed frozen in every season. Barry said a woman stood in it at night, facing the wall, humming a tune with no ending. It followed him for thirty years.

1971Coudersport, PA

The first haunting.

His parents blamed drafts and an overactive imagination — until the humming started coming from inside the walls. Then it started coming from Barry himself, in his sleep, in a voice that wasn't his. The family moved twice before he turned twelve. It didn't matter. Whatever stood in the cold spot came with them each time — a little closer, a little louder.

1998Chittenden, VT

It had waited. It had followed.

Barry grew up, married, and swore he'd left it behind. He raised three sons and told them nothing. Then a job moved the family to a restored colonial in Vermont, and he felt it the first night he crossed the threshold: the upstairs hall went cold, and somewhere in the dark a woman began to hum. Now there were four Ravencrafts under one roof for it to reach.

1998–2000The investigation

Two years, out of pure survival.

Barry's three sons — Bret, Brock, and Blake — spent two years recording, photographing, and researching their own home just to endure it. By the time they understood what they were dealing with, they'd taught themselves to investigate the unexplained better than anyone they could have hired.

2019The humming stopped

It moved on the night he did.

Barry passed in 2019, and whatever had followed him since he was seven went quiet the same night — on to wherever he did. His sons kept the recordings, the photographs, the field notes, and one hard rule their father had drilled into them.

What the boys saw.

Bret · the eldest

Every door, open.

He woke to find every door in the house standing open — and the humming coming from all of them at once.

Brock · the photograph

Her face on the film.

He photographed the cold spot on a dare, and developed film showing the woman's face — though no one had been standing there.

Blake · the youngest

The only one she spoke to.

She spoke to him directly. He never told anyone what she said. He still won't.

File 02 // Approach

We rule out the ordinary before we accept the extraordinary.

Most "hauntings" have an explanation — a draft, a loose pipe, low-frequency sound you can feel but not hear, even carbon monoxide. We start there. What's left over, when the ordinary is ruled out, is what we investigate carefully and document honestly.

Principle 01

Debunk before belief

Every reading is measured against a documented baseline. If there's a natural cause, we find it and we tell you — that's a successful investigation, not a failed one.

Principle 02

Chain of evidence

Cameras, audio, and instruments run continuously with synced timestamps. Nothing is cut, staged, or "enhanced." You see exactly what we recorded.

Principle 03

No fear, no judgment

We don't provoke, we don't perform, and we never sell you dread. Whatever you've experienced, you'll be taken seriously and treated with respect.

File 03 // Methodology

How an investigation runs.

A repeatable, documented sequence — the same one the brothers built inside their own home, whether we're standing in your hallway or coordinating the case from a thousand miles away.

01
Intake & interview

You tell us what's happening.

We gather the history of the property, a timeline of events, who has experienced what, and the questions you actually want answered. No detail is too small or too strange.

02
Baseline & walkthrough

We document the property's normal.

Before dark, we map electromagnetic fields, temperatures, airflow, sound, and structure — so any anomaly has something real to be measured against.

03
The investigation

The instruments go live.

Overnight, on-site or remotely coordinated. Cameras roll, audio runs on multiple channels, and readings log continuously — every event stamped to the second.

04
Analysis

We review every hour.

Footage and audio are examined frame by frame and cross-referenced against the instrument logs. We separate the explainable from the genuinely unexplained.

05
Findings & cross-over

You get a clear, written report.

Plain-English conclusions and the evidence behind them. Where activity appears genuine, we talk through respectful next steps — including helping a lingering presence move on.

File 04 // Instrumentation

The kit we carry.

Consumer ghost apps make noise; instruments make data. Everything below is deployed against a baseline and recorded — so a reading means something.

EM Field

Map electromagnetic fields and flag anomalies against the documented baseline of the room.

Optical

Full-spectrum & IR cameras

See past visible light — infrared and ultraviolet — recorded to timestamped DVR across the site.

Thermal

Thermal imaging

Track sudden cold spots and heat signatures a room has no ordinary reason to produce.

Audio

EVP & digital recorders

Capture electronic voice phenomena across multiple recorders so anything caught can be cross-referenced.

Field

REM pod & ambient probes

Detect real-time changes in the radiating field and air temperature around a fixed point.

Motion

Laser grid & trip sensors

Reveal movement and shadow crossing a mapped plane of light the eye can't track in the dark.

Vision

SLS / structured light

Flag humanoid figures that standard optics and the naked eye consistently miss.

Safety

Environmental baseline kit

Barometric, humidity, and carbon-monoxide readings — because CO can mimic a haunting, and it's dangerous.

File 06 // Standards

What we will never do.

The Ravencrafts spent thirty years on the other side of the phone call. Trust in this field is earned by what you refuse to do — and these lines don't move.

✕

We are not a religious or cleansing service

No exorcisms, no curse-lifting, no channeling for hire. If you want spiritual guidance, we'll help you find it — we don't sell it.

✕

We won't manufacture a haunting

Drafts, wiring, infrasound, and carbon monoxide explain most cases. If there's a natural cause, that is our honest finding.

✕

We never charge fear

No pressure, no invented follow-ups, no upsells built on someone's worst night. You'll always know the cost before we begin.

✕

We won't expose you

Nothing about your case — your name, your address, your footage — is shared or published without your written permission.

File 07 // Questions

Before you reach out.

Do you charge for this?

Every case starts with a free intake conversation. On-site investigations and remote reviews are quoted only after we understand the scope — and we'll never invent a reason to keep coming back.

What if you find nothing?

That's a real and valuable result. Most reports rule out natural causes — bad wiring, drafts, infrasound, carbon monoxide — and that alone can bring peace, and sometimes safety.

Is any of this dangerous?

No. Our methods are observational — we watch, measure, and record. There's no provocation and no theatrics. Your family's comfort comes first, every time.

Can you really investigate remotely?

Yes. We coordinate camera and recorder placement, monitor and log the session live with you, then review the evidence. It's how we reach cases anywhere in the world.

Will anyone find out?

Not from us. Confidentiality is the default. Nothing about your case is shared or published without your written consent — no exceptions.

Do you work with skeptics?

Especially skeptics. We are skeptics — we simply document carefully before we draw any conclusion. Doubt is welcome in the room.
